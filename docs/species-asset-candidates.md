# Cat and rabbit models: status

## 2026-11-12: original Blender models replace the procedural and scan assets

Both atlases are now generated by `scripts/blender/` (see
`public/models/AUTHORED-SPECIES.md`): a parametric skeleton with named
landmarks and every muscle built as its own mesh from its attachments, layered
from deep to superficial. The cat ships 234 muscles and the rabbit 222 (both
sides). The viewer offers layer-by-layer peeling, isolation, focus and search.

Removed from the shipped app:

- the Sketchfab cat and rabbit skeleton scans (CC BY-NC and CC BY-NC-SA, not
  compatible with commercial cohort licences);
- the Feline Ecorche sculpt (CC BY 4.0; one fused surface whose muscles could
  not be selected individually).

They may still be used locally as visual references only; nothing derived from
their geometry is shipped.

## Anatomical references

Muscle lists, attachments, actions and innervation follow standard veterinary
myology: University of Minnesota carnivore anatomy labs and Penn State cat
dissection material for the cat; the Clemson rabbit muscular atlas
(Mukhopadhyay & Wagner), rabbit hindlimb architecture studies and comparative
rabbit anatomy for the rabbit. These sources inform names and relationships,
not vertex coordinates; their illustrations are not redistributed or traced.

## Open items

- Independent review by a veterinary anatomist of every muscle's attachments,
  layer and species naming before the models are sold as teaching material.
- Small intrinsic paw muscles, cutaneous trunci, facial and tongue muscles are
  not modelled.
- Poses use the existing illustrative rig and need review for the new geometry.
