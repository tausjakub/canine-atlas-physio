## Interpretation rules

- **Explicit motion** means the printed text identifies the moving segment and its direction. A drawing may reinforce this.
- **Inferred plane** is an anatomical interpretation for visualization. The source pages do not formally name sagittal, dorsal or transverse planes.
- A dot marks therapist contact, and many arrows describe hand pressure or tissue glide. They must not all become skeletal movement arrows.
- A named technique, an alternate patient position, a contact variant and a later movement phase are different things. They need distinct labels in the UI.
- No joint angles, force magnitudes, displacement distances or speeds are specified here. Do not present authored animation values as measured ranges or source instructions.
- Preserve the PDF's numbering, including its duplicate **14**. Do not silently renumber subsequent topics.

## Implementation coverage checklist

The axial material supports these **separate presentations**, rather than ten generic flexion loops:

1. Pelvic contact synchronized to hindlimb extension, with a pressure-free return.
2. Pelvic standing-position alternative with the PDF contact markers distinguished from limb motion.
3. Sacral landmark orientation and midline/edge tissue glide on stable bone.
4. Sacral contact during hindlimb movement: lying and standing alternatives.
5. Sacral decompression with lateral tail movement: crest and margin contact variants.
6. Selected caudal vertebra fixation with distal lateral tail movement and separate traction cue.
7. Lumbar and thoracic spinous-process contact during unspecified body movement; any selected motion direction explicitly authored/inferred.
8. Cervical side-to-side movement with C6 contact, then distinct C5-C2 contact/level variants.
9. Atlas anterior-surface contact/tissue work with no invented skeletal motion.
10. Occipital-edge contact during a head nod.
11. AO head nod with the source's separate support and contact/traction cues.
