## Full movement audit update (2026-10-22)

The original four animations were a limited selection, not a full inventory.
Every complete page has now been reviewed again across three recorded audits:
[overview and plane conventions](dorn-movement-audit-overview.md),
[limb topics](dorn-movement-audit-limbs.md), and
[pelvis, spine and head](dorn-movement-audit-axial.md).

The study section now covers all **22 numbered source topics**, including the
source's variants and the repeated number 14 for sacral decompression. It has
**nine illustrative 3D movement studies**: the original stifle, elbow, carpus
and forepaw toes, plus hip fore/aft motion, hip external rotation, hock bending,
shoulder forward excursion and hindpaw toes. These counts are different:
one topic can include multiple motions, and a contact/tissue topic need not
define an independently animated bone motion.

The forepaw digit source has been corrected to **page 25 / topic 12**;
**page 18 / topic 6** now has its own hindpaw model. Anatomical planes and axes
are explicitly identified as added explanations. Technique pages do not name
formal movement planes or provide measured 3D axes. In particular, page 32
does not establish the direction/plane of trunk movement, and the atlas
contact on page 34 does not itself prescribe head nodding.

Reviewed source: user-supplied `dormova metoda pro psy.pdf`, 73,655,691 bytes,

`2f25c20e7632ea7c03ae278bc8a0c96bc163a212d5f7b6e093264b4e9fa8a66e`.

does not repair these, add OCR, flatten graphics, or infer missing content.

## What the illustrations establish

The study diagrams can explain the **direction** of flexion, extension and rotation and
the change between selected poses. They do not establish angular range, end feel,
contact pressure, traction force, speed in seconds, patient suitability or a
therapeutic dose. The display clock is a playback control, never a clinical timer.
No digit spreading, forceful end-range stretching, corrective joint repositioning,
traction or spinal manipulation should be invented for the animation.

Page 9 says movements are slow and continuous and mentions a maximum of 15
repetitions. Pages 13 and 26 show red `15-20` ranges without a unit; several other
pages show ranges such as `10-15`. The app deliberately avoids resolving this
conflict into a treatment prescription. Source-page previews keep the printed
numbers visible as part of the original document, with the animation separately
identified as a direction illustration.

Pages 3-6 emphasize examining the animal before treatment, recognizing abnormal
temperature, swelling/sensitivity and pain responses, adapting to the animal, and
seeking further professional help where needed. There is **no comprehensive
contraindications chapter** in this PDF. This manual alone cannot be labelled a
complete screening guide or independently validated efficacy source. Statements
about correcting bone position or joint dysfunction are the manual's claims,
not verification performed by the app.

## Import and integration

- Library id: `dorn-method-dogs`; language `cs`; category `manual-therapy`.
- Parts: 1-6, 7-12, 13-18, 19-24, 25-30, 31-35; all below 20 MiB.
- Original page-preview JPEGs: `/library/dorn-preview-13.jpg`, `-15.jpg`, `-17.jpg`,
  `-18.jpg`, `-19.jpg`, `-20.jpg`, `-23.jpg`, `-24.jpg`, `-25.jpg`. These preserve
  full-page headings, captions, arrows and source footer.
- Reader uses global PDF page indices and converts to the correct local part page.
  It includes manual-topic shortcuts, direct page entry and a link to `?view=dorn`.
- Library labels identify this source as Czech and scanned; text search is not
  advertised for image-only pages.
- `scripts/import-dorn-pdf.py` preserves encoded PDF image bytes, dimensions,
  rotation and decoded drawing streams and verifies every imported page against
  its source. It is repeatable without duplicating the library entry.
- Raw PDF files and rendered previews are sensitive course material too: the
  server access gate must protect `/library/` before they are served. Importing
  the files does not itself provide access control or permission to redistribute.
