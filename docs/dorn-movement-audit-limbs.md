## Interpretation rules

- No page in this range explicitly names a sagittal, dorsal/frontal, or transverse anatomical plane. Any such plane label in the app must be identified as **anatomical explanation added to the source**, supported separately. A side-view drawing is not itself proof that all depicted forces and movement share one plane.
- The source's filled contact dots and arrows can describe a therapist's hand, pressure, traction, or return. These must not all become translations/rotations of the animal's bones. Each entry below distinguishes segment movement from manual input.
- The red parenthetical numbers have no visible unit on these pages. Do not reinterpret them as degrees, speeds, safe ranges, or an app exercise prescription.
- The source's joint classifications below are reported as its wording, not independently endorsed anatomical claims.
- Pages 13-18 form the pelvic-limb run; pages 19-25 form the thoracic-limb run. Page 18 and page 25 use near-identical generic toe headings/artwork, but occupy distinct numbered positions in those runs. Consequently the current forepaw animation linked to page 18 mismatches the source context. Keep **hind digits -> page 18 / topic 6**, and **fore digits -> page 25 / topic 12**.
- Source illustrations do not specify quantitative axes, exact bone-centre trajectories, or patient-specific ranges. A 3D illustration must say which motions are simplified interpretations.
