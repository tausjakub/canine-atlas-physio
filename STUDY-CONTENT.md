# Exam and practical study content

The Exam prep tab retains its 13 topics, 58 criterion mappings and existing quizzes. Each criterion now has an additional bilingual explanation. Each topic adds a fictional case reasoning prompt, an answer and a common mistake. A separate 58-item closed-book recall checklist records self-review, independently of quiz scores.

The Practical / Praxe tab has 12 stations: five home observation/voluntary-handling rehearsals, four rehearsals without a dog, and three instructor-led learning areas. Each has setup, three steps, observation guidance, stop criteria, an oral explanation, three preparation checks, relevant exam links and a PDF-page link. Illustrations are labelled as generated context images; existing anatomy photographs and conceptual diagrams retain their own credits and limitations.

Scope mapping was checked against NSK qualification 43-001-R on 9 September 2026 (standard valid from 21 October 2022). The qualification includes dogs and cats. Coverage of the criteria is not a guarantee of exam readiness or a complete clinical textbook. Independent veterinary peer review is still outstanding. Clinical examination, manual techniques, reflex-induced training, aids and continence care require supervised teaching.

Original explanations draw on the supplied Millis/Levine and Goldberg/Tomlinson books and the topic references already linked in the application. The astrophysics book remains in the library but is not presented as a veterinary source. PDF links use file page numbers, not printed numbering. Further sources for the home-handling boundary include Cornell cooperative-care guidance and AAHA nonpharmacologic pain-management guidance, linked in the Practical footer.

No individual dog health history was provided. Home stations therefore allow only suitable familiar activity, respect existing veterinary restrictions, and provide video/model alternatives. There are no universal exercise doses, device settings, home pain-perception tests or bladder-expression instructions.

Local storage keys:

- `canine-atlas-criterion-recall-v1`: validated stable topic/criterion IDs.
- `canine-atlas-practice-rehearsal-v1`: validated station/check IDs.

Storage is local to the browser and origin. Rehearsal checks do not certify live-animal competence. A failed read/write is surfaced in the UI.

Validation: `scripts/validate-practice.mjs` checks complete bilingual criterion coverage, cases, station structure, reference targets, all station renders in both languages and base paths, storage behavior and lesson navigation. The GitHub Pages asset validation includes all three new WebP assets.
