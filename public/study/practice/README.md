# Practical preparation illustrations

Three original AI-generated editorial illustrations, created with OpenAI image generation in September 2026 and visually inspected for the depicted external handling context. WebP delivery copies are 1536 × 1024 pixels.

- `calm-approach.webp`: voluntary approach to an open hand on a nonslip mat.
- `gentle-touch.webp`: light surface contact at the shoulder-side region, without joint movement.
- `walking-observation.webp`: ordinary walking observed by another person with a camera.

These depict a setting, not anatomical detail, a diagnostic examination or a therapeutic technique. They must not be used as evidence of exact anatomy, limb-loading percentages or a prescribed movement pattern. The station text and visible image captions communicate these limits. For the natural-stand and transition stations, the text explicitly distinguishes the depicted setup from the observation task.
