## Rabbit — skeleton-scan.glb

"Squelette de lapin (rabbit skeleton)" by Philippe C (actarus83).
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/squelette-de-lapin-rabbit-skeleton-06b78bc7560d4f108441ee7e5975abe0

Licensed CC BY-NC 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

The user supplied squelette_de_lapin_rabbit_skeleton.glb, copied unchanged.
Source attribution and license are also embedded in asset.extras. The author
describes photogrammetry of an approximately 40 cm mounted skeleton, with
manually reconstructed ribs and dorsal spines. Exact species is not established
in the source description. Three technical meshes do not correspond to named
bones. One 24-vertex display plinth is hidden by default, with a visibility control.

## Presentation and limitations

Display changes: normalized framing, rabbit rotation, lighting, optional ivory
material in place of original textures, optional display-plinth visibility.
Source GLB files remain unchanged. Attribution and license links appear beside
the models. These licenses do not permit commercial use. Adaptations of the cat
asset must retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; this does not relicense independent app code.

These are scanned skeletons, not complete musculoskeletal atlases. No muscles
are included and no unsupported per-bone picking or pose deformation is applied.
The separately authored study schematic remains a distinct mode. Its notes and
muscles have not been spatially registered to these scan meshes.
